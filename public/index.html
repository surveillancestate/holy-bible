About the KJV

The King James Version (KJV), also known as the Authorized Version (AV), is an English translation of the Christian Bible for the Church of England. It was commissioned in 1604 and published in 1611. The KJV is in the public domain in most countries, making it freely available for use and distribution.

This application provides a clean, modern interface for reading the timeless words of the King James Bible. Navigate through books and chapters using the reader above.

Features

  • 📖 All 66 books of the Bible (39 Old Testament, 27 New Testament)
  • 🔍 Easy navigation between books and chapters
  • 💾 Bookmark your favorite verses (requires login)
  • 🌙 Dark mode support (coming soon)
  • 📱 Responsive design for mobile devices
  • 🔐 OAuth authentication via Google or GitHub
